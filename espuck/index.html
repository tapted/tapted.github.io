espuck

Board Game Tracker Updates

Keep your espuck running the latest firmware for flawless turn and point tracking. Connect your device via USB to flash the latest system image directly from your browser.

Note: Requires Chrome, Edge, or Brave. Safari and Firefox do not support Web Serial.

How to Update

  1. Plug the espuck device into your computer using a data-capable USB cable.
  2. Click the Connect button above.
  3. Select the USB serial port from the popup (usually labelled "USB JTAG/serial debug unit", "CP210x", or "CH340").
  4. Click Install espuck and wait for the process to complete.

Troubleshooting & Drivers

If you don't see a COM port when you click Connect, your computer might be missing the necessary USB drivers.
